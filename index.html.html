Risk Framing · Why This Matters Now

Bad automation scales failure
faster than service.

Two international cases that became governance benchmarks

✈️
Air Canada
Hallucination · Legal liability · 2024

Chatbot fabricated a bereavement fare refund policy. Customer relied on it. Airline was found legally responsible for misinformation generated by its own bot — without content guardrails or knowledge grounding.

Hallucination No content audit Reputational damage
📦
DPD Parcel Delivery
Behavioral failure · Viral · 2024

After a system update, the bot insulted a customer and criticized its own company publicly. DPD was forced to disable the AI component. The interaction went viral in hours — no behavioral guardrails, no tone controls, no confidence thresholds.

No tone guardrails Missing confidence filter Bot disabled urgently
💡 The pattern is identical: knowledge gaps + no behavioral controls + weak escalation logic = automation becomes brand risk, not efficiency.

Pillar C
Feedback Loop · BSAT Strategy

Dissatisfaction should trigger action,
not a monthly report.

Layer 1 — Signal Capture

Tag architecture from Pillar A becomes the BSAT early warning system. Every negative rating, loop, and cold escalation tagged in real time.

Layer 2 — Data Pipeline

Zendesk API → Python/Pandas → Looker Studio. Conversation-level friction view: which use case, which intent, which KB article correlates with BSAT drops.

Layer 3 — Action Loop

Identify friction → fix → deploy → validate with post-delivery metrics. A/B test higher-risk changes with curated cohort of top partners.

Continuous improvement engine
📡
Detect — BSAT signal or friction tag fires
🔍
Diagnose — root cause: content? prompt? API? routing?
⚙️
Fix & Deploy — targeted use case or KB update
✅
Validate — post-delivery metrics confirm improvement
📊 Zendesk AI reporting tracks BSAT and escalation at use-case level — exactly the lens this loop requires.
